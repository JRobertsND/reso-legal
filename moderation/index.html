Moderation & Removal Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Purpose

This policy explains how Reso reviews reports, removes content, and restricts accounts to keep the community safe and lawful.

What we moderate

  • Content and behavior that violate our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.
  • Illegal content and copyright-infringing material under our Copyright / DMCA Policy.
  • Spam, malware, and security threats.

How decisions are made

  • We may rely on user reports, automated signals, and human review.
  • Actions can include content removal, feature limits, warnings, temporary locks, or permanent bans.
  • Severity, history, and intent may affect the outcome.

Account deletion and removal requests

  • You may delete your account in Settings; associated content is removed according to our Privacy Policy and technical processes.
  • We may retain limited records when required for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons.

Appeals

  • If you believe we made an error, contact Support with details about the content or account action.
  • We aim to review good-faith appeals, but we are not obligated to reverse every decision.

Transparency

  • We may update this policy as our processes evolve. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Contact

Moderation questions: Settings → Report a Problem or Contact Support.
