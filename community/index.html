Community Guidelines

Last updated September 29, 2026

Our community

Reso is built for musicians to create, share, and collaborate. These guidelines explain what is and isn’t allowed so everyone can create safely.

Be respectful

  • Treat others with respect in posts, comments, messages, and collabs.
  • No harassment, hate, bullying, threats, or unwanted sexual content.
  • No doxxing or sharing someone else’s private information.

Keep it legal and safe

  • No illegal activity or content that promotes serious harm.
  • No graphic violence or sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance).
  • No spam, scams, or deceptive engagement schemes.

Respect creative rights

  • Only post music and media you have the rights to use.
  • Credit collaborators appropriately; don’t claim others’ work as your own.
  • Report copyright issues using our Copyright / DMCA Policy.

Authentic accounts

  • Don’t impersonate others or misrepresent your identity in a misleading way.
  • Don’t operate networks of fake accounts to manipulate the community.

Collaboration norms

  • Honor collab invites and don’t misuse private drafts or unreleased material shared with you.
  • Don’t harass creators who decline an invite or collaboration.

Enforcement

  • We may remove content, limit features, or suspend accounts that break these guidelines.
  • See our Moderation & Removal Policy for how decisions are made and how to contact us.

Reporting

  • Use in-app reporting tools when available, or Settings → Report a Problem / Contact Support.
