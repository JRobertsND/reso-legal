Copyright / DMCA Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Overview

Reso respects intellectual property rights. This policy explains how to submit a copyright infringement notice and how we respond.

Reporting infringement

If you believe content on Reso infringes your copyright, send a notice including:

1. Your contact information (name, address, email, phone). 2. A description of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed. 3. A description of the material you claim is infringing and where it appears on Reso (URL, username, post details). 4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law. 5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act on the owner’s behalf. 6. Your physical or electronic signature.

Where to send notices

Email copyright notices to copyright@resomusic.app with subject line “Copyright / DMCA Notice.”

Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification with the information required under applicable law (including DMCA §512(g) if you are in the United States).

Repeat infringers

  • Accounts that repeatedly infringe may be suspended or terminated.

Contact

Copyright questions: copyright@resomusic.app or Settings → Contact Support.
